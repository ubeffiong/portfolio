Ubokobong Okon Effiong

Digital Health · Public Health Informatics · Bioinformatics & Computational Biology · Global Health Security

I am a digital health leader with 8+ years building, deploying and scaling the systems Nigeria's health sector runs on. That includes hospital information systems in 25+ facilities, an informatics dashboard at the Federal Ministry of Health, the architecture of NHEx (the National Health Exchange), FastCare telemedicine, CareWallet payments, and LiSphere laboratory connectivity. I have trained 4,500+ health workers to use these systems.

In public health, I lead technical work on Nigeria's national surveillance system (SORMAS) and architected DAVT (the Data Analytics and Visualization Tool), now used in all 36 states and the FCT. With a B.Sc. in Biochemistry and an M.Sc. in Public Health, I am also both a bioinformatics analyst and developer. I analyse genomic data and build reproducible tools for plasmid-mediated antimicrobial resistance (PlasBench, PlasmiCord, PlasmidTrace), so that genomic surveillance is something public-health teams can trust and act on.

About

Digital health, from the hospital ward to the national level

Digital health is the thread through my career: making health systems work digitally, from one hospital ward to national exchanges and ministry dashboards. I started in frontline hospital-software support and worked up to technical leadership, while also running laboratory information systems in a tertiary hospital and leading national surveillance work. Alongside that I do computational biology research on plasmid-borne antimicrobial resistance. That mix means I can talk with clinicians, laboratory scientists, epidemiologists, bioinformaticians, executives and engineers, and turn what they need into systems that work.

What I bring

Digital health leadership

Digital health products from strategy to scale: HMIS/EMR in 25+ facilities, the FMoH informatics dashboard, NHEx, telemedicine, health payments and insurance, with roadmaps, go-live, training and continuous improvement.

Public-health informatics

National surveillance optimisation, case-investigation workflows, data pipelines, outbreak thresholds, automated reporting and data quality.

Enterprise interoperability

HMIS/EMR, LIS, analysers, PACS/DICOM, telemedicine, payments and national exchange concepts using FHIR, REST, ASTM and HL7.

Laboratory informatics

Built a hospital LIS with bidirectional links to Abbott, Roche Cobas, Selectra and Sysmex analysers; founder of LiSphere Connector (460+ instrument models).

Bioinformatics & computational biology

Plasmid reconstruction benchmarking, sequence-distance analysis, plasmid clustering, AMR–plasmid linkage and transmission-confidence scoring for genomic surveillance.

Training, governance & adoption

Competency-based curricula, mentoring, SOPs, version control, change control and sustainable handover to government teams.
