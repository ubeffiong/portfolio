Bioinformatics & biosecurity direction

My genomics work sits on plasmid-mediated antimicrobial resistance: benchmarking how reliably tools reconstruct plasmids, finding plasmids shared across bacterial lineages that chromosome-only surveillance misses, and attaching honest confidence to transmission signals. I treat sequence similarity as evidence to weigh, never as proof of transmission.

I want to contribute an African implementation perspective to the safe convergence of bioinformatics, AI and genomic surveillance. That means reproducible and auditable pipelines, governance of AI-enabled biological analysis, responsible data sharing, and equitable participation of African scientists in biosecurity policy and innovation. Upcoming Selected participant, SPREAD Hackathon 2026 (Surveillance of Plasmid-borne Resistance: Education, Analysis and Development), Copenhagen, November 2026.
